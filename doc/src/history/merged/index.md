title: Arb, Calcium and Antic
summary: The version histories of the libraries that were merged into FLINT 3.0: Arb (ball arithmetic), Calcium (exact real and complex numbers) and Antic (number fields).
order: 3
---

Arb, Calcium and Antic were developed as separate libraries on top of
FLINT 2 and merged into FLINT 3.0 in 2023, at versions Arb 2.23,
Calcium 0.4 and Antic 0.2.5; their modules (`arb`, `acb`, `ca`,
`qqbar`, `nf_elem`, `qfb`, ...) kept their names. The pages below give
their release notes up to the merge, newest first.
