## FLINT 3.7.0 (in development) {#flint-3-7-0}

### Performance

* Replace GMP's `mpz_gcd`, `mpz_gcdext` and `mpz_invert` in `fmpz_gcd`,
  `fmpz_xgcd`, `fmpz_invmod` and related functions by ports of GMP's
  half-GCD code using FLINT's multiplication and FFTs. GCDs are
  typically 1.1x faster than with GMP 6.3.0 up to 1000 limbs and 2x
  faster at $10^6$ limbs, extended GCDs up to 3x faster. Adds
  `flint_mpz_gcd`, `flint_mpz_gcdext`, `flint_mpz_invert`, `flint_mpn_gcdext`,
  `_flint_mpn_gcd_inplace` and `_flint_mpn_gcdext_inplace` [FJ].
* `flint_mpn_gcd_full` is renamed `flint_mpn_gcd` and handles small
  operands faster. `flint_mpn_gcd_full` and
  `flint_mpn_gcd_full2` remain as deprecated macros [FJ].
* Faster contents of integer vectors (`_fmpz_vec_content` and the
  contents and canonical forms of `fmpz_poly`, `fmpq_poly`, `fmpz_mat`,
  `fmpz_mpoly`, `fmpq_mpoly` and `fmpz_mpoly_q`), using divisibility tests
  instead of chained GCDs: 10x to 75x faster for long vectors of
  single-limb entries with a content other than 1. Adds
  `flint_mpn_gcd_vec_1` and `flint_mpn_gcd_vec` [FJ].
* Faster LCMs of integer vectors and clearing of denominators
  (`_fmpz_vec_lcm`, `_fmpq_vec_get_fmpz_vec_fmpz`, the
  `fmpq_mat_get_fmpz_mat` functions and their uses in `fmpq_poly` and
  `gr`): for 1000 denominators that are 1, equal or divisors of a common
  denominator, the LCM is 5x to 18x faster and the clearing of
  denominators 2x to 6x faster. Adds `_fmpz_vec_lcm_strided` and
  `_fmpq_vec_get_fmpz_vec_scaled` [FJ].

### Bug fixes

* Fix the modular inverse in the curve selection of `fmpz_factor_ecm`,
  which was wrong when its cofactor had fewer limbs than the modulus
  (wasting the curve), and which could call GMP's `mpn_gcdext` with its
  operands in the wrong order [FJ].

* Fix crashes in finite-field multivariate Wang and Zippel factorization
  when an evaluation gives incompatible leading coefficient divisors
  [[#2905](https://github.com/flintlib/flint/issues/2905)].

* Fix `arb_pow` losing containment when the output aliases an inexact
  exponent and the base interval has a zero endpoint
  [[#2879](https://github.com/flintlib/flint/issues/2879)].
