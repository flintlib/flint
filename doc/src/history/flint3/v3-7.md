## FLINT 3.7.0 (in development) {#flint-3-7-0}

### Performance

* Replace GMP's `mpz_gcd`, `mpz_gcdext` and `mpz_invert` in `fmpz_gcd`,
  `fmpz_xgcd`, `fmpz_invmod` and related functions by ports of GMP's
  half-GCD code using FLINT's multiplication and FFTs. GCDs are
  typically 1.1x faster than with GMP 6.3.0 up to 1000 limbs and 2x
  faster at $10^6$ limbs, extended GCDs up to 3x faster. Adds
  `flint_mpz_gcd`, `flint_mpz_gcdext`, `flint_mpz_invert`, `flint_mpn_gcdext`,
  `_flint_mpn_gcd_inplace` and `_flint_mpn_gcdext_inplace` [FJ].
* `flint_mpn_gcd_full` is renamed `flint_mpn_gcd` and handles small
  operands faster. `flint_mpn_gcd_full` and
  `flint_mpn_gcd_full2` remain as deprecated macros [FJ].
* Faster contents of integer vectors (`_fmpz_vec_content` and the
  contents and canonical forms of `fmpz_poly`, `fmpq_poly`, `fmpz_mat`,
  `fmpz_mpoly`, `fmpq_mpoly` and `fmpz_mpoly_q`), using divisibility tests
  instead of chained GCDs: 10x to 75x faster for long vectors of
  single-limb entries with a content other than 1. Adds
  `flint_mpn_gcd_vec_1` and `flint_mpn_gcd_vec` [FJ].
* Faster LCMs of integer vectors and clearing of denominators
  (`_fmpz_vec_lcm`, `_fmpq_vec_get_fmpz_vec_fmpz`, the
  `fmpq_mat_get_fmpz_mat` functions and their uses in `fmpq_poly` and
  `gr`): for 1000 denominators that are 1, equal or divisors of a common
  denominator, the LCM is 5x to 18x faster and the clearing of
  denominators 2x to 6x faster. Adds `_fmpz_vec_lcm_strided` and
  `_fmpq_vec_get_fmpz_vec_scaled` [FJ].

* Rewrite the reverse Bernoulli iterator (`bernoulli_rev_init`,
  `bernoulli_rev_next`): numbers in batches of 16 from one pass over the
  power table, prefactor products with shared FFT transforms, a tiled
  table kernel (and an AVX-512 IFMA kernel where available), threads
  inside each batch, a table built in parallel without temporaries and
  shrinking as the iteration proceeds, and for $n \ge 10^5$ a table
  sieved by 3 and 5 (about 40% less memory). `bernoulli_rev_struct` now
  holds a pointer to a private state (an ABI change); the iterator
  throws above `BERNOULLI_REV_NMAX` [FJ].
* Compute Khinchin's constant (`arb_const_khinchin` and the new
  `mp_real_const_khinchin`) from exact Bernoulli numbers, direct sums with
  recycled powers and a quadratic form in logarithms of primes, in
  parallel: orders of magnitude faster than before at high precision
  (one million digits in about a quarter of an hour on 8 threads) [FJ].
* From about $1.5 \cdot 10^6$ digits, compute Khinchin's constant by an
  approximate functional equation for the dilogarithm series of Bailey,
  Borwein and Crandall, with $O(\sqrt n)$ terms of quasilinear cost
  instead of power sums of cubic cost: $10^7$ digits in 11.4 hours on 8
  threads, 5.7x faster than with the zeta-value sum. Adds
  `_mp_real_const_khinchin_afe`, `mp_real_gamma_upper_half_frac` and
  `MP_REAL_KHINCHIN_AFE_CUTOFF` [FJ].
* `mp_real_exp`: evaluate short dyadic arguments by the bit-burst
  algorithm instead of a full-length argument reduction (for example 7x
  faster for a 64-bit argument at 51907 limbs) [FJ].
* Add `mp_real_hypgeom_sum`, the binary splitting of
  `mp_real_hypgeom_series` as a public function [FJ].
* `arith_bernoulli_number_denom` enumerates the divisors of $n$ instead
  of scanning the primes up to $n$ [FJ].
* Implement `FLINT_PARALLEL_DYNAMIC` scheduling in `flint_parallel_do`
  (previously the same as `FLINT_PARALLEL_UNIFORM`) [FJ].
* Add `mp_real_pow_ui` [FJ].
* `fmpz_mat_hnf_pernet_stein`, and with it `fmpz_mat_hnf` and
  `fmpz_mat_snf`, no longer recurse one row at a time on input whose
  determinant spreads over many small elementary divisors: when the
  largest elementary divisor fits in a word, the HNF is computed
  modulo a multiple of it. For a 652 x 652 matrix with elementary
  divisors 1, 2 and 6, `fmpz_mat_snf` takes 5 s instead of 10 min [MH].
* `fmpz_mat_solve_dixon_den` (used by `fmpq_mat_solve_fmpz_mat`) no
  longer switches from word-size to $p^k$ digits far too early when a
  few rows of the matrix have much larger entries than the rest. For a
  $119 \times 119$ matrix with one row of 28000-bit entries and two
  right-hand sides, the solve is 35x faster
  [MH, [#2947](https://github.com/flintlib/flint/pull/2947)].

### Bug fixes

* Fix the modular inverse in the curve selection of `fmpz_factor_ecm`,
  which was wrong when its cofactor had fewer limbs than the modulus
  (wasting the curve), and which could call GMP's `mpn_gcdext` with its
  operands in the wrong order [FJ].

* Fix crashes in finite-field multivariate Wang and Zippel factorization
  when an evaluation gives incompatible leading coefficient divisors
  [[#2905](https://github.com/flintlib/flint/issues/2905)].

* Fix `arb_pow` losing containment when the output aliases an inexact
  exponent and the base interval has a zero endpoint
  [[#2879](https://github.com/flintlib/flint/issues/2879)].

* Fix `arb_pow` returning nan for a small exact integer exponent when
  the base has a zero midpoint and a nonzero radius
  [[#2908](https://github.com/flintlib/flint/issues/2908)].
